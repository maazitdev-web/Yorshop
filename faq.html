Orders & Checkout

Browse our products, select the items you like, add them to your cart and proceed to checkout. Enter your delivery details to complete your order.

Visit the My Orders page to view your saved order details and order history.

Please contact our support team as soon as possible to request an order cancellation.

Payments

Cash on Delivery (COD) is currently available. Online payment options are not yet integrated.

Cash on Delivery means you pay for your order when it arrives at your delivery address.

Shipping & Delivery

Delivery time depends on your location and the shipping arrangements for your order. Please check our Shipping & Delivery page.

You can view your saved order details through the My Orders page. Live courier tracking is not currently integrated.

Returns & Refunds

Returns may be possible depending on the product and its condition. Please read our Returns & Refunds policy for details.

Contact our support team and explain the issue. Include clear photos of the damaged product when possible.
