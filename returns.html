Returns & Refunds

Find out how to request a return and get help with a refund at YourShop.

We're Here to Help

Your satisfaction matters to us. If something isn't right with your order, please review the information below and contact our support team for assistance.

1. Return Eligibility

Return requests may be considered when a product arrives damaged, defective, incorrect, or does not match the order details. Eligibility will depend on the product and the circumstances of the request.

2. Return Conditions

  • The product should be in its original condition.
  • Original packaging and included accessories should be retained.
  • Proof of purchase or order details may be required.
  • Products showing misuse or accidental damage may not qualify.

3. How to Request a Return

01

Contact Support

Contact our team with your order number and a description of the issue.

02

Request Review

Our team will review your request and explain the next steps.

03

Return Instructions

If approved, you'll receive instructions for returning the product.

04

Resolution

We'll explain the available resolution, such as a replacement or refund.

4. Refund Process

Once an approved return has been received and reviewed, we will communicate the applicable refund process. Refund methods and processing times depend on the payment method and the arrangements available.

5. Damaged or Incorrect Products

If you receive a damaged, defective, or incorrect item, contact us as soon as possible. Include your order details and clear photographs of the product when appropriate so we can review the issue.

6. Non-Returnable Products

Certain products may have special return restrictions for hygiene, safety, or other legitimate reasons. Any applicable restrictions should be communicated before purchase and must follow applicable consumer protection laws.

7. Frequently Asked Questions

Can I return a damaged product?

Contact support with your order details and photographs so the issue can be reviewed.

How can I request a refund?

Contact our support team. They will explain the return and refund process.

Where can I get help with my order?

Visit our Contact Us page and send us your question with the relevant order details.

Need Help With a Return?

Our support team is ready to help you with your order concerns.
