Important Delivery Information

  • Always provide a complete and accurate delivery address.
  • Include a valid phone number so delivery staff can contact you if necessary.
  • Delivery may take longer during holidays, busy shopping periods, or unexpected delays.
  • Someone should be available to receive the package at the delivery address.
  • Please contact our support team if you experience a delivery problem.
