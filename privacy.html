Your Privacy Is Important to Us

At YourShop, we respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal information. This policy explains how information may be collected and used when you visit or use our website.

Last updated: November 10, 2026

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use YourShop, we may collect:

  • Name and contact information.
  • Email address and phone number.
  • Shipping and billing details.
  • Order and shopping history.
  • Messages submitted through our contact form.

2. How We Use Your Information

Information may be used to:

  • Process and manage customer orders.
  • Provide customer support.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Communicate important order updates.
  • Help prevent fraudulent activity.

3. Cookies and Website Data

Cookies and similar technologies may be used to improve website functionality and understand how visitors use our website. You can manage cookies through your browser settings.

4. Data Protection

We aim to use appropriate security measures to protect personal information. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not intend to sell customers' personal information. Information may need to be shared with service providers when necessary to operate the store, process orders, or meet legal obligations.

6. Your Privacy Choices

You may contact us to ask about the personal information associated with you or request corrections, subject to applicable laws and our ability to verify your request.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or practices change. Any updated version will be published on this page.
